V3 WALL SENTENCEState is not relevance.

The world can keep changing while behavior looks elsewhere. A fast-changing question need not rewrite the state whose job is to stay ready for future questions.

Scientific fence: eight deterministic seeds are not a significance study; the teacher is synthetic; the vanilla RNN is not the strongest recurrent baseline; and Fusion fit training better. The next gate must close that optimization confound with loss-matched checkpoints and stronger gated attackers.
Claim boundary: v0–v3 are synthetic computational experiments. They are not biological-neuron claims, not evidence that dendrites literally store arbitrary programs, and not evidence that basket/chandelier cells implement the exact software decomposition. Biology motivates sharper interventions; checked-in receipts are the scientific authority.
